Returns & Shipping

Shipping, returns, and support policy for ThermoX orders.

1. Shipping Coverage

ThermoX ships from Pretoria to addresses within South Africa. If you need shipping outside South Africa, contact us before placing your order so we can confirm options and pricing.

2. Processing and Delivery Times

  • Orders are typically processed within 1 to 3 business days after payment confirmation.
  • Delivery time depends on destination and courier service area.
  • Tracking details are provided once your order has been dispatched.

3. Shipping Fees

Shipping fees are shown at checkout where applicable. Promotions such as free shipping above a minimum order value may be offered from time to time.

4. Returns Policy

  • Returns may be requested within 30 days of delivery.
  • Items must be returned in good condition with all included accessories where possible.
  • To begin a return, email support with your order details and reason for return.

5. Warranty Support

ThermoX provides warranty support for manufacturing defects. Warranty claims are reviewed case-by-case. Damage caused by misuse, modification, or accidental damage is not covered.

6. Refunds

Once a returned item is received and inspected, we will confirm whether a refund or replacement is approved. Approved refunds are processed to the original payment method, subject to payment provider timelines.

7. Contact For Shipping and Returns

For shipping, returns, or warranty support, contact thermox.service@gmail.com.
