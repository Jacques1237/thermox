Returns & Shipping

Example policies for how ThermoX orders could be shipped and returned.

1. Shipping (Demo)

In this demo, shipping costs and delivery estimates are examples only. The checkout mentions free shipping above a certain order value, but no actual logistics or carriers are integrated yet.

2. Example Live Shipping Policy

  • Orders dispatched within 2–3 business days once payment clears.
  • Free shipping on qualifying orders above a specified amount.
  • Tracking details provided by email once the order ships.

3. Returns (Demo)

The ThermoX product page references a 30‑day guarantee and a 1‑year limited warranty as example terms. In this demo, no real returns are processed.

4. Example Live Returns & Warranty Policy

  • 30‑day return window from date of delivery for unused or lightly used units.
  • 1‑year limited warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.
  • Customer responsible for return shipping unless the product is DOA or mis‑shipped.

5. Contact For Support

For questions about how a real ThermoX shipping and returns process would work, contact support@example.com.
