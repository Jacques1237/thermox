Privacy Policy

How data entered into this ThermoX demo is handled.

1. Demo-Only Data Handling

This ThermoX site runs as a front-end demo. Information you enter into forms (name, email, address, etc.) is only stored locally in your browser using localStorage so that the checkout and order confirmation screens can be demonstrated.

2. No Server-Side Storage

In this demo configuration, no personal data is sent to, or stored on, a remote server. Closing your browser or clearing your browser storage will remove the demo order data saved on your device.

3. Cookies and Tracking

This prototype does not intentionally use cookies or third-party tracking scripts. When the store goes live, an updated Privacy Policy will describe any analytics or tracking tools in use.

4. Future Live Store

When ThermoX goes live as a real online store, this policy will be updated to explain what data is collected, how it is used, and how long it is retained, as well as your rights under applicable privacy laws.

5. Contact

For questions about how this demo handles data, you can reach the project maintainer at support@example.com.
